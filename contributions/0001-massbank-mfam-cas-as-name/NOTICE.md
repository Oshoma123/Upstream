# Third-party data

The files in `original/` and `patched/` are MassBank records from
[MassBank/MassBank-data](https://github.com/MassBank/MassBank-data) (`mFam/`,
branch `dev`, retrieved 2026-10-01). They are licensed **CC BY** by their
authors, as stated in each record's `AUTHORS` and `LICENSE` lines:
Gerd Balcke, IPB Halle, Germany.

`patched/` differs from `original/` only as shown in `change.diff`. The
project's MIT and CC BY 4.0 terms do not cover these files.
